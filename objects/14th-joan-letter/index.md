# Letter from Joan I of Aragon
King Joan I of Aragon wrote this letter to his wife, Violant de Bar, on November 8, 1391. In it, Joan thanks Violant for sending him a small box of perfumes that he says helped him convalesce and regain good health. He goes on to express his gratitude for her counsel on matters of state. Joan addresses Violant as *molt cara companyona*, my dearest companion, at the beginning of each sentence of the letter. Before delivering it, chancery scribes copied the letter into a register along with copies of with other letters sent under Joan's secret seal. The word *secret* most likely corresponds to the modern use of the word *private*.
